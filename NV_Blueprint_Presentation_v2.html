Legal Structure
Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs)
For major productions and co-founded ventures, NV creates a Single-Project LLC — a Special Purpose Vehicle. This keeps each project financially separate from the main company and allows outside investors to participate cleanly.
  • ▸
    Protects Next VisionProject-specific risk stays inside the SPV — doesn't touch NV's main assets
  • ▸
    Enables Project InvestorsInvestors can buy into a specific film or venture without taking equity in all of NV
  • ▸
    Clean DistributionRevenue from that project distributes to SPV members only — no company-wide complication
  • ▸
    When We Use ThemMajor film productions, large events, co-founded businesses, and international partnerships

Tulsa Film Tribe
The Tulsa Film Tribe is being formed as an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit — not a subsidiary of NV, but a sister organization. This distinction matters: it keeps NV's agency business separate from the collective's community mission, and unlocks grant funding and nonprofit benefits.
  • ▸
    Independent 501(c)(3)Its own legal identity — eligible for grants, donations, and public charity status
  • ▸
    NV's Talent PipelineFilm Tribe members are the first call for NV projects — community builds the workforce
